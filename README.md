# Brittania's Relationship with Thrace During Its Decline

---

The world, as many children would honestly acknowledge and any adult past their rebellious attitudes would repent in the name of, is based a great deal on human relationships.

Brittania, starting at around the time of its decline and exacerbated by decisions such as Brexit (see [Changissnz/changes_in_law_and_order_in_america__claude_generated](https://github.com/Changissnz/changes_in_law_and_order_in_america__claude_generated) for more on that), was intent on maintaining its vision for relationships to others. This fact was, by no means an achievement of Brittania but a necessity for it during its adjustment in strategy. Its world was shrinking. India was no longer delegating its command over to Brittania after it realized that the resource-dependent nature of the Brittanian kernel could not sacrifice to impose stress on working relations any further, especially since the economic order in that time period was not working like clockwork. Brittania's difficulties in maintaining influence with Asia Major through Hong Kong was already mentioned in the story [Changissnz/brittania_sure_loves_its_bubbles__claude_generated](https://github.com/Changissnz/brittania_sure_loves_its_bubbles__claude_generated).

Resource constraints and growing costs of transportation had reprioritized regionalism as a greater initiative. And in this trend, Brittania strove to have the greater say through dealing with some of its neighboring challengers. A commendable challenger was Thrace. At that point in time, Thrace was no longer an official nation. But it was, in fact, still a numerous ethno-realm under the multiple umbrellas of Brittanian, Gaullian, and Germanian identity. The Thrace of that time period was not entirely that during the Ancient Roman era, but there were nevertheless still strong associations between those peoples of that present time to those of the past. Brittania, despite its use of softer tools such as financial instruments, was still a much more consolidated and better trained force than Thrace, which had a significant portion of its population involved in paganism and lesser civility, in orthodox terms. Brittania wanted to matter more when it came to Thrace. Brittania wanted to matter in general, as [Changissnz/brittania_wanted_to_matter__claude_generated](https://github.com/Changissnz/brittania_wanted_to_matter__claude_generated) showed. It was on the retreat in several domains, but it wanted Thrace to still know it was above it at the main discussion table.

Brittania attempted to alleviate risk by administering its own version of the populist trends that were occurring in the United States. The populism of the Brittanian kernel was different to that in the United States. Its populism was just as reactionary, but the flavor was also more keen on passive differentiation than the firebrand flavor of the United States. In turn, Brittania maintained an unspoken but obvious orthodoxy in its appearance and mannerisms. It defamiliarized itself with Schinian and Indian orthodoxies, to the point where interaction with those peoples was becoming alien to them in the same way that Brittania first made contact with the two subcontinents. Brittania had members of Schinia and India residing in its lands, even in its kernel. But the cultural ignorance arose once again in ways that encouraged members of both sides to feign ignorance of the others' expectations, in forms such as commerce and security deals. In lay speak, it became typical for a Brittanian of the kernel to politely say things such as "wow, so that is how you Schinians do things" and "fascinating, so that is how you Indians think and speak". Statements such as those demonstrated an acknowledgement of these different cultures along with some rather uninviting undertones to those peoples' causes.

Brittania's skirmishes with Thrace were not all documented, and the little documentation provided on it tended to mask the identities of the political actors under more contemporary language. These skirmishes comprised at least a third of the far-right power struggles mentioned in works such as [Changissnz/second_quarter_of_the_21st__so_much_for_the_age_of_reason__claude_generated](https://github.com/Changissnz/second_quarter_of_the_21st__so_much_for_the_age_of_reason__claude_generated). Those on the outside saw the differentiation occurring because of the nature of these events.

Thrace was, in simple terms, a wild and carefree demographic that could be considered a mixture of Gypsy cousins, Germanian outlanders, and Gaullian miscreants. Neither Brittania nor Thrace had outward centralized command. Brittania had retained that trait since the decades of its ceding power over to the Continental United States. But it, underneath, still acted by organized methodologies and was no novice in the art of perfect execution through technological amplification, telecommunicated coordination. This proved challenging for Thrace, which had come to depend on Schlavia for much of its technological needs. This dependency came at the cost of eroding its cordial relations with the leading members of anti-Schlavian Europe.

Brittania made the systematic defeat of Thrace go on repeat during these mini skirmishes. Its military leaders justified the mini skirmishes as necessary due to the close proximity of a challenging Thrace to the Brittanian kernel, and because of Thrace's subservient role as a commercial trader, meaning that antagonizing Thrace would not bear as much financial damage to the Brittanian kernel.

From the outside, there were amused members witnessing Brittania antagonize one of its close relatives. Many members, especially not of the Eurozone, were completely oblivious to these mini skirmishes. Casualties were not as great as those of Judea in Judea's confrontations with its Arabian nemeses. These unreported fights helped Brittania to maintain its militant edge, conditioning and maintaining its executive strength. They also encouraged Brittania to become more insular on the world stage.
